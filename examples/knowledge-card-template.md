# Knowledge Card Template

## Company strategy format

### The crux

One sentence describing the central strategic mechanism.

### What works

1. **Mechanism:** why it creates or protects value.
2. **Mechanism:** why it compounds or scales.
3. **Mechanism:** what organizational capability enables it.

### What does not work

1. **Constraint:** where the strategy is vulnerable.
2. **Constraint:** what the company cannot copy or change easily.
3. **Constraint:** what external shift could weaken the advantage.

### Said vs. Read

- **Said:** a source-grounded claim with its private timestamp reference.
- **Read:** a clearly labeled inference derived from the source.
