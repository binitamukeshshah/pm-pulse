# Privacy, Rights, and Source Handling

PM Pulse is designed for private personal study. The public repository documents the workflow and product decisions; it does not publish the private learning library.

## Public repository boundaries

This repository must not contain:

- podcast or subscription transcripts;
- paid research articles;
- generated summaries that reproduce substantial source expression;
- private Q&A or learning history;
- provider credentials or session tokens;
- email addresses, private drive links, or account identifiers; or
- audio files downloaded for local transcription.

## Authorized source routes

PM Pulse uses only:

1. official transcripts;
2. text supplied by the user from a legitimate account or subscription; or
3. local speech-to-text applied to legitimately accessible public audio.

If no authorized route exists, the item is not processed.

## Attribution and inference

- Source provenance is recorded in the private guide.
- Direct quotations are checked against the transcript.
- Analysis distinguishes what the source **Said** from what the system **Read** or inferred.
- Outside knowledge is labeled rather than blended into source-grounded answers.

## No redistribution grant

The presence of workflow documentation in this repository does not grant rights to third-party podcasts, transcripts, articles, brands, or subscription material. Users are responsible for complying with the terms and rights that govern their own source access.
